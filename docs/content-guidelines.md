# Content guidelines

## What SeatsConnect is

API-powered **B2B distribution infrastructure** for tickets, hospitality and live experiences.

It connects:

- **Supply** — venues, promoters, hospitality providers, approved suppliers
- **Demand** — travel, concierge, corporate, hotels, sports travel, white-label / digital partners

…through one controlled infrastructure layer.

## What SeatsConnect is not

Do not describe SeatsConnect as:

- a consumer ticket marketplace
- a resale platform
- a broker
- SeatsBrokers (or any SeatsBrokers reference)

## Preferred terminology

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| distribution infrastructure | marketplace |
| approved partners / channels | anyone / open public |
| controlled distribution | unrestricted inventory dump |
| Search → Quote → Book → Fulfil | “buy tickets now” |
| connect inventory | list tickets for sale |
| B2B / professional | fan / consumer |

## Primary CTAs

1. **Connect Your Inventory** — suppliers
2. **Join Our Network** — distribution partners
3. **Talk to Our Team** — sales / general
4. **Login** — existing partners → `https://app.seatsconnect.com`

## Claims policy

- Do not invent supplier counts, partner counts, countries, ticket volumes, or API latency.
- Do not show venue / league / partner logos unless `launchFlags.partnerLogos` is true **and** permission exists.
- Legal pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/cookies`, `/compliance`) remain placeholders until counsel provides final wording.

## Tone

Professional, infrastructure-led, clear. Techy UI is fine; copy should stay B2B and controlled — not hype marketplace language.
